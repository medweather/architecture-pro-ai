# HyperRelay

HyperRelay is a fictional interstellar communication technology in the
Stellar Chronicles franchise. It allows near-instant transmission of data
across star systems.

The HyperRelay network is powered by a Void Core energy cell, which supplies
the immense power required to keep the relay stable. Without a Void Core,
a HyperRelay cannot sustain a stable signal and collapses within minutes.

HyperRelay stations are maintained by the Aether Guard and monitored by the
Interstellar Coalition. During the Replicant Wars the network was a primary
target for the Void Cabal.
