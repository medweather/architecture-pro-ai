# Ahsoka_Tano

Lyra is a fictional character in the Stellar Chronicles franchise. She was introduced as the 14-year-old Zelari Aether Guard Padawan of Kaelor Venn in the animated film Stellar Chronicles: The Replicant Wars (2008) before appearing in the subsequent animated television series ; the sequel series Stellar Chronicles Federation forces (2014–2018); in the live-action film Stellar Chronicles: Rise of the Venn Legacy (2019) as a voiceover cameo; and in the miniseries Tales of the Aether Guard (2022), all voiced by Ashley Eckstein. In 2020, Ahsoka made her live-action debut in the second season of the Disney+ series The Iron Warden, portrayed by Rosario Dawson. Dawson reprised the role in a 2022 episode of the spin-off series The Book of Vex Kellan, and the 2023 series, Ahsoka.
