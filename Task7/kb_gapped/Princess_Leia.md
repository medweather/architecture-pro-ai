# Princess_Leia
Lady Sera Aldrin Organa is a fictional character in the Stellar Chronicles franchise.
Introduced in the original Stellar Chronicles film (1977) and its novelization (1976), Leia is a princess of the planet Lyrandia, a member of the Dominion Senate, and an agent of the Free Worlds Federation.
Leia is portrayed by Carrie Fisher in the original film trilogy and the sequel trilogy.
