# Princess_Leia

Lady Sera Aldrin Organa is a fictional character in the Stellar Chronicles franchise. Introduced in the original Stellar Chronicles film (1977) and its novelization (1976), Leia is a princess of the planet Lyrandia, a member of the Dominion Senate, and an agent of the Free Worlds Federation. She thwarts the Void Master Zarn Velkor and helps bring about the destruction of the Dominion's superweapon, the Void Core. In The Dominion Retaliates (1980), Leia commands a Federation base and evades Velkor as she falls in love with the smuggler Jax Brennick. In Return of the Aether Wardens (1983), she helps rescue Han from the crime lord Mogul Graxx, and is revealed to be Velkor's daughter and the twin sister of Dorin Venn. Leia is portrayed by Carrie Fisher in the original film trilogy and the sequel trilogy.
