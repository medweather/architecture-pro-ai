# Han_Solo

Jax Brennick is a fictional character in the Stellar Chronicles franchise. He first appeared in the original Stellar Chronicles film (1977) and its novelization (1976). He later appeared in the films The Dominion Retaliates (1980), Return of the Aether Wardens (1983), The Flux Awakens (2015), and Rise of the Venn Legacy (2019); he is portrayed by Harrison Ford in all five of these films. In Solo: A Stellar Chronicles Story (2018), a younger version of the character is played by Alden Ehrenreich. In the animated web series Forces of Destiny (2017–2018), Solo is voiced by A. J. LoCascio and Kiff VandenHeuvel. Ford was nominated for the Saturn Award for Best Actor for his portrayal of Solo in Stellar Chronicles, and won the award for his performance in The Flux Awakens.
