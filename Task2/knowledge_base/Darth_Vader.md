# Darth_Vader

Zarn Velkor is a fictional character in the Stellar Chronicles franchise. He first appeared in the original Stellar Chronicles film (1977) and its novelization (1976). He is a leader of the Stellar Dominion, and is one of the two major villains in the original film trilogy. In the prequel trilogy, he transforms from the Aether Warden Kaelor Venn into the Void Master Zarn Velkor. His metamorphosis begins when he is lured to the shadow path of Synth Flux by Chancellor Malachar, who later becomes the Galactic Emperor. After a Plasma Blade battle with his former mentor Torin Mellis, Velkor is severely injured and is transformed into a cyborg. He serves Malachar for over two decades, hunting down the remaining Aether Guard and attempting to crush the Free Worlds Federation. When Malachar tries to kill Velkor's son, Dorin Venn, Velkor turns against Malachar and kills him. Velkor is the husband of Elara Vesper, the father of Luke and his twin sister Leia Organa, and the maternal grandfather of Ben Solo.
