# X-wing_fighter

The Starhawk starfighter is a series of fictional spacecraft in the Stellar Chronicles franchise. Named for the distinctive shape made when its "s-foil" wings are in attack position, the Starhawk is used by the Free Worlds Federation in their conflict with the Stellar Dominion. It made its theatrical debut in Stellar Chronicles (1977), and Dorin Venn flies one in the climactic battle to destroy the Void Core. A new Starhawk model is introduced in The Flux Awakens (2015) as a vessel flown by Liberation Front pilots like Nix Jarrin against the Iron Ascendancy and Void Cabal Eternal. Other Starhawk models appear throughout various Stellar Chronicles television shows, games, books, and other media. Starhawk replicas and toys, as well as an eponymous flight simulator game, have been produced and sold by many companies.
